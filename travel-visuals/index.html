Behind the frame

Curiosity takes the scenic route.

I’m an emerging travel and adventure photographer based in Karnataka, India. I’m interested in the feeling of a place as much as its postcard view: the roads that lead there, the people who shape it, and the light that changes it by the minute.

I’m building a body of work focused on landscapes, road journeys, and visual storytelling for destinations and brands. I bring a considered eye for framing, a love for the journey, and a collaborative approach to every assignment.
